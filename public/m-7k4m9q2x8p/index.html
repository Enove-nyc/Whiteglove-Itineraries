    Where does the information come from?+

    LL152 schedules come from the NYC Department of Buildings covered-property list, updated July 27, 2026 and retrieved November 6, 2026. Address matching uses NYC Planning’s GeoSearch. Backflow testing requirements come from NYC DEP.

    DEP: Backflow prevention ↗

    Does a deadline mean my building is overdue?+

    No. A scheduled deadline does not show whether an inspection has already been completed. This version does not verify individual filings, extensions, no-gas certifications, or new-building deferrals. Older filings may also be absent from the DOB NOW public portal.

    Why might a backflow due date be unavailable?+

    DEP requires device testing every 12 months, but we have not identified a public citywide source for individual device test dates. Without a verified test record, we cannot determine your device’s exact deadline. A property may have more than one device.

    What if my property isn’t listed?+

    A missing match can reflect alternate addresses, multiple buildings on one lot, a changed classification, or a gap in the published list. It does not establish an exemption. LL152 information is matched to a building identification number (BIN), rather than an individual apartment.
